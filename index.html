~/path-planner

    
A small browser version of my path planner. The real one makes time-optimized
      autonomous routes for VEX U robots with Hermite splines. Click the field to add points,
      drag them to move them, then hit run path.

    

      
      

        
        
        
      

      
The real project is Python. Cubic and quintic Hermite splines, with forward and
        backward passes to keep velocity and acceleration in limits. Code:
        github.com/maxsr0/path-planner.
